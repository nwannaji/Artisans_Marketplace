## 1. Introduction

FixIt App ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, FixIt App (the "App"), and our related services.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you register or use the App:

- **Name** — Your first and last name as provided during registration
- **Email address** — Used for account verification, notifications, and communication
- **Phone number** — Used for verification, OTP authentication, and service-related notifications
- **Profile picture** — Optional photo you upload to your profile
- **Address** — Your physical address (collected from customers for service delivery)

### 2.2 Location Information

We collect and use your location information:

- **Precise location (GPS)** — Used to show nearby artisans on the map and for service delivery routing
- **Approximate location** — Used for general area matching when precise location is unavailable
- **Location data is only collected while you are using the App** (foreground access only)

### 2.3 Device and Audio Information

- **Microphone access** — The App requests microphone permission for voice messaging or recording features related to service communication. Audio recording is optional and only activated when you explicitly choose to use it.
- **Network information** — We check network connectivity to ensure reliable service delivery

### 2.4 Artisan-Specific Information

If you register as an artisan, we also collect:

- **Profession and skills** — To match you with relevant service requests
- **Hourly rate** — Your service pricing
- **Verification documents** — Government-issued ID or professional certifications for identity and qualification verification
- **Availability status** — Whether you are available, busy, engaged, or offline

### 2.5 Service and Booking Information

- **Job listings and booking details** — Service descriptions, scheduling, agreed prices, and job status
- **Ratings and reviews** — Feedback left by customers and artisans
- **Communication records** — Messages exchanged between customers and artisans within the App

> **Note:** FixIt is a booking and discovery platform. Customers and artisans agree on service prices within the app, but all payments are handled directly between the parties outside the app. FixIt does not process, hold, or facilitate any payments, escrow, or financial transactions.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not sell** your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

### 5.1 Service Providers

We share information with the third-party providers listed above solely to provide the services described in this policy.

### 5.2 Between Users

- **Customers** can see an artisan's name, profession, skills, rating, location (approximate), and availability status
- **Artisans** can see a customer's name, address (for service delivery), and job details

### 5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

### 5.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- **Encrypted data transmission** — All API communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Secure authentication** — Token-based authentication with secure storage on device
- **Database security** — Server-side access controls and encrypted storage
- **OTP verification** — Time-limited one-time passwords for sensitive operations

> **Disclaimer:** No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
